# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS), deployed on Vercel. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Emily, an 8-year-old girl in Colombia (Spanish is her first language), practicing at home on a tablet, phone, or computer, sometimes alone and sometimes with her uncle. Her uncle (Diego, an English teacher) builds and adds activities for her over time.

## Product Purpose

A private, playful learning place made just for Emily. It hosts interactive games and practice activities: first multiplication tables (1 to 10) and English practice, with more sections added later. Success means Emily wants to come back, plays without needing an adult to explain the screen, and gets visibly better at the tables and English.

## Positioning

Made for one specific child: her name, her interests, and activities her uncle writes for what she is learning right now, instead of a generic app full of ads, accounts, and levels to buy.

## Operating Context

- Used on touch screens and on a computer equally: every control must work with a finger and with mouse/keyboard.
- An 8-year-old reader: few words, big targets, icons and pictures that explain themselves.
- Sessions are short (5 to 15 minutes).
- Progress, if kept, lives on the device only (no accounts, no backend).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interface language (buttons, instructions, feedback): English, by the user's choice, kept very simple so the site itself is English exposure.
- Multiplication tables: 1 to 10.
- English practice content: open; vocabulary, phrases, and listening at an early primary level. Emily recently did a "show and tell" about her vacation in Cartagena (past simple: went, ate, swam, saw...).
- New sections will be added over time, so the home must grow without redesign.
- No login, no ads, no external tracking, no collection of personal data.

## Brand Commitments

- The site is about Emily and uses her name.
- The user asked for a style for an 8-year-old girl: very colorful, with flowers of many colors.
- The title must say this is Emily's fun, interactive place to learn (user asked Claude to propose it).

## Evidence on Hand

No logos or brand assets. Vacation photos exist outside this repo; none are part of the site yet.

## Product Principles

1. Emily understands every screen without an adult reading it to her.
2. Mistakes are safe and kind: encourage, show the right answer, never punish.
3. Every activity gives instant, joyful feedback.
4. Easy for her uncle to add a new game or section.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large touch targets, high-contrast text, audio support (speech) for English words, respects reduced-motion settings.
